
# What Even Is AI?

Welcome to the world of AI! Let's start with the basics.

## What Is AI?

AI is software that understands language and generates responses.

When you type a message, the AI predicts what words should come next based on patterns it learned from billions of sentences.

## How Does AI Work?

When you send a message to an AI, it uses patterns learned during its training to predict what words should come next.

It generates text based on what statistically makes sense according to those learned patterns.

## Does AI Actually Know Things?

The key insight: **AI doesn't "know" things the way you do.**

Instead, it generates text that statistically makes sense based on its training.

This means AI doesn't necessarily understand the information it provides in the same way a human does. It generates responses based on patterns it has learned.

## Why Does How You Ask Matter?

Because AI generates responses based on patterns and the information you provide, the way you phrase your message matters.

Different ways of asking can lead to different responses.

This is why **how you ask matters**, and that's exactly what prompt engineering is all about.
